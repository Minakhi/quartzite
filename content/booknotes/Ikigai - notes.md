Ikigai = something you're good at, that you love doing, that the world needs, and that you can get paid for.  
  
Dedicating your life to an Ikigai prolongs your life. Happiness and contentment are easier to have.  
  
Process, not product.  
  
Go slow. Don't hurry. Be aware. Be present.  
  
Being in motion throughout the day is more important than exercising for an hour in the morning.  
  
Never retire. If you've found your Ikigai, for what reason would you not want to do it to your dying day?  
  
A sense of community of shared values helps you live longer. Don't overburden your partner with all expectations. Keep a set of friends as well.  
  
Premeditatio Malorum. And then, be antifragile.
